Questions

Before you upload

How do I sign in?

With your registered email. We send a 6-digit code to that address, valid for a few minutes. There is no password to remember.

Can I get a refund on my wallet?

No. Money added to the wallet is not refundable and cannot be withdrawn or transferred; it can only be used for reports. A report charged in error, or a payment that was debited but not credited, is corrected.

Do you store the PDF password?

No. It is used once to open the file and is not saved.

Which banks are supported?

{{ BANK_COUNT }} banks, listed on the supported banks page. If a layout is not recognised you get a clear error and pay nothing.

How long are reports kept?

Excel files are kept for {{ RETAIN_DAYS }} days. Upload the statement again to regenerate one.

Does the account holder need to consent?

Yes. You must have the account holder's consent before you upload their statement, and you confirm this at sign-up and with every upload. You are responsible for that consent and for how you use the report.

Is this a credit decision?

No. {{ BRAND }} is a software platform. It reads the PDF you upload and computes figures from it. It does not verify the statement with the bank and does not take credit decisions. Check the report against the original statement before you rely on it.
